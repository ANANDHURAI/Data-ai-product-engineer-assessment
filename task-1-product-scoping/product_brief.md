# Marketing Performance Dashboard

## Problem

Marketing analysts currently collect data manually from different tools like Google Ads and Meta Ads.

This process:
- takes time
- creates inconsistent reports
- depends heavily on specific team members

The company needs a simpler and faster way to understand marketing performance across channels.

---

## Proposed Solution

Build an internal dashboard that combines marketing performance data into one place.

The dashboard helps analysts quickly answer:
- Which channel performs best?
- Which campaigns are underperforming?
- Where should marketing focus next?

---

## Primary Users

- Internal marketing analysts
- Account managers

---

## Core Goal

Reduce manual reporting work and provide faster, more consistent insights.

---

## Success Criteria

A user should be able to:
- view marketing performance quickly
- compare channels
- identify trends
- make decisions faster

## Data Sources

The dashboard will rely on data exported from existing marketing platforms such as:

- Google Ads
- Meta Ads
- Google Analytics
- LinkedIn Ads

For V1, data will be uploaded manually through CSV exports to avoid disrupting the team's current workflow.
